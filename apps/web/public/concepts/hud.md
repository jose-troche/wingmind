# Head-up display

The pitch ladder rolls with the horizon; the circle with wings is the **flight-path marker**, where the aircraft is actually going. Airspeed is on the left, altitude on the right, heading across the top.

- **BREAK** arrow: ORCH's missile-defense directive, pointing to the beam side.
- **PULL UP** chevrons: the ground-collision system predicts terrain inside 150 ft on a recovery that starts two seconds from now. With protection on, it flies the recovery and gives control back once clear.
- G and angle of attack turn amber, then red, as you approach the limits.
