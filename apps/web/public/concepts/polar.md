# Signature polar plot

Your radar signature depends on the **aspect** a radar sees: the frontal sector is smallest, the beams spike. The green line is the 36-bin signature table; each needle is a known enemy radar at the angle it sees you.

Detection range grows with the **fourth root** of signature, so a tenfold cut in signature shrinks a radar's reach by about 44 percent.

- Turn and watch the needles cross the lobes.
- Keeping a needle near the nose keeps that radar's detection ring small.
