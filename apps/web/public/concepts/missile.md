# Missile defense

MAWS correlates a **plume** from the distributed aperture with a **guidance-mode emitter** on the warning receiver. Both on one bearing means a radar-guided missile; a plume alone means an infrared missile.

The defense is a **beam** turn: put the missile at your 3 or 9 o'clock so its radar sees the least closing speed, and dispense the decoy that matches its seeker.

- Radar missile: break to the beam, chaff, radar silent.
- Infrared missile: flares, break, out of afterburner.
- NAV picks the beam side that does not turn into rising terrain.

The whole chain, from plume to the first audio, runs inside one sim frame plus audio start: under 150 ms.
