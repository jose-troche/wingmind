# Warning receiver

The scope shows radars illuminating you, by bearing relative to the nose. Distance from the centre shows the mode, not the range:

- **Outer ring**: search. They are looking.
- **Middle ring**: track. They have you.
- **Inner ring, flashing**: guidance. A missile may be in the air.

Letters: S long-range SAM, F fighter, U unknown. The diamond marks the strongest emitter.
