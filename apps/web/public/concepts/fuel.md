# Fuel

**Bingo** is the fuel needed to get home plus a reserve; **joker** is bingo plus 2,000 lb, the point to start thinking about home. SYS recomputes both from your position every second.

The engines feed from the forward tank; the wings transfer to the aft tank and the aft tank tops up the feed, keeping the centre of gravity in limits.

- Say "fuel state" or "time to bingo" for a spoken answer.
- The red mark on the bar is bingo, the amber mark joker.
