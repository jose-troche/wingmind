# Situation display

North is up and your aircraft is in the centre. Tracks are FUSE's fused picture, not ground truth: they can be late, noisy or missing.

- Diamond: aircraft. Triangle: missile. Square: drone. Circle: unknown.
- Red shaded polygons: SAM threat rings cut by terrain.
- Dotted circles: where each enemy radar can detect you at your current aspect.
- Blue line: your route. Dashed green: a proposed re-route waiting for "accept".
- Amber circles: thunderstorm cells.
